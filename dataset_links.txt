Dataset Sources
===============

1. Stroke Risk Dataset (structured/numeric-categorical)
   File: stroke_risk_dataset.csv
   Source: Kaggle - Stroke Risk Prediction Dataset
   https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mahatiratusher/stroke-risk-prediction-dataset-v2

2. Drug Reviews Dataset (text)
   Files: drugsComTrain_raw.csv, drugsComTest_raw.csv
   Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository - Drug Review Dataset (Drugs.com)
   https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/462/drug+review+dataset+drugs+com
   Also mirrored on Kaggle:
   https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/jessicali9530/kuc-hackathon-winter-2018
